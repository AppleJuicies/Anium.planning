Privacy policy

Anium.planning · Updated October 6, 2026

Anium.planning is a planning workspace that runs in your web browser. There is no account to create, no database, and no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. We never receive the projects you make.

What is stored, and where

  • On your device. Your projects, pictures and settings (such as theme, font and the last project you had open) are saved in your browser's storage on your own device. They stay there until you clear this site's data.
  • In your Google Drive, if you connect it. Your projects are saved as files in a folder in your own Google Drive, so they sync between your devices. Files you attach to tasks are uploaded to the same folder. The app also keeps a status report (a Google Doc) there and saves any status slides you create. All of it belongs to you and stays in your Drive.

Google Drive access

If you choose Connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for one permission: drive.file. This lets the app create files and open only the files it created itself. It cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.

Your files travel directly between your browser and Google; they never pass through our site.

So you only have to sign in once on each browser, the site keeps your Google sign-in in a single encrypted cookie in your browser. Page scripts can't read it, and it lasts up to 400 days. When the app needs Drive access, a small sign-in service on this site uses that cookie to get a one-hour access pass from Google. The service doesn't store anything: no database, no copies of your sign-in, and no logs of your files. Choosing Sign out in the app deletes the cookie and cancels the app's access with Google.

Anium.planning's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to save and load your own projects. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you turn it on. If you turn on Anyone with the link can view for a project, anyone with that link can view that one project and download a copy of it. Your other projects stay private. Turning sharing off stops the link working.

Services the site relies on

  • Vercel hosts the website and its sign-in service. Like any web host, it may log technical details such as your IP address. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google's servers.
  • Google sign-in and Google Drive are used only if you connect Drive or open a shared link. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Picture addresses you paste. If you add a picture by its web address, your browser loads it from that website.

Removing your data

  • To sign this browser out, choose Sign out next to Google Drive in the app.
  • To remove what's on this device, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • To remove what's in Drive, delete the app's folder from your Google Drive.
  • To remove the app's access to your Drive, go to your Google Account's third-party access page and remove Anium.planning.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, open an issue at github.com/AppleJuicies/Anium.planning.
