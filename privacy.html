Privacy policy

Anium.planning · Updated October 8, 2026

Anium.planning is a day planner that runs in your web browser: an hour-by-hour schedule for each day, a timeline of your longer tasks, and a record of what you finished. There is no account to create, no database, and no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. We don't keep copies of your planner.

What is stored, and where

  • On your device. Your days, tasks and settings (such as theme and the view you had open) are saved in your browser's storage on your own device. They stay there until you clear this site's data.
  • In your Google Drive, if you connect it. Your planner is saved as one file, planner.json, in an “Anium.planning” folder in your own Google Drive, so it syncs between your devices. Files you attach to tasks go into an “Attachments” folder inside it. The status report (a Google Doc) and any status slides you make are saved in the same folder. All of it belongs to you and stays in your Drive.

Google Drive access

If you choose Connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for one permission: drive.file. This lets the app create files and open only the files it created itself. It cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.

Your planner and files travel directly between your browser and Google; they don't pass through our site.

So you only have to sign in once on each browser, the site keeps your Google sign-in in a single encrypted cookie in your browser. Page scripts can't read it, and it lasts up to 400 days. When the app needs Drive access, a small sign-in service on this site uses that cookie to get a one-hour access pass from Google. The service doesn't store anything: no database, no copies of your sign-in, and no logs of your files. Choosing Sign out in the app deletes the cookie and cancels the app's access with Google.

Planning with Claude

Plan my day is optional and only runs when you press Plan it with Claude. It sends what you typed, along with that day's schedule, unfinished blocks from your last planned day, and the names and dates of your open timeline tasks, to Anthropic's Claude model through Vercel AI Gateway. Claude replies with a suggested schedule, which you review before anything changes.

To use it you must be signed in with Google, so the site knows the request comes from a real user of the app. Our site doesn't store what's sent or the reply, and nothing is used to train AI models on our behalf. Vercel and Anthropic handle the request under their own policies (linked below).

Anium.planning's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to save and load your own planner and, when you ask for it, to plan your day. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Services the site relies on

  • Vercel hosts the website, its sign-in service and the planning service, and runs Vercel AI Gateway. Like any web host, it may log technical details such as your IP address. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Anthropic provides the Claude model used by Plan my day. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Google sign-in and Google Drive are used only if you connect Drive. See Google's privacy policy.

Removing your data

  • To sign this browser out, choose Sign out in the app's Google Drive menu (top right).
  • To remove what's on this device, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • To remove what's in Drive, delete the “Anium.planning” folder from your Google Drive.
  • To remove the app's access to your Drive, go to your Google Account's third-party access page and remove Anium.planning.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, open an issue at github.com/AppleJuicies/Anium.planning.
